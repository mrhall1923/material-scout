How this was checked. Prospects came from each library's full catalogue (Poly Haven: all textures; ambientCG: every material), chosen by eye from the thumbnails. The licence line was read from each prospect's own page, fetched again on 2026-10-16: every Poly Haven page shows “CC0 License” beside the author credit; every ambientCG page says “All assets are released under the Creative Commons CC0 license…”. A prospect whose page lacked the line would have been dropped. ambientCG's technique is copied from the page's Technique field (the site spells procedural “Prodedural”). Poly Haven pages have no technique field; its textures are scanned from real surfaces, and where credits name a photographer that is shown.

Real size is the area the photo covers. Poly Haven: the asset's own dimensions (its API), with the “… wide” figure its page prints. ambientCG: the page's Dimensions line; where the page gives none, the catalogue's figure, or “not given”. 2K size is the download a pull would make: for Poly Haven the five 2K PNG maps tools/surface_photos.py fetches (colour, OpenGL normal, roughness, AO, ARM), with the JPG total beside it; for ambientCG the 2K-PNG zip that tool fetches, with the 2K-JPG zip beside it. Sizes are from the libraries' APIs; nothing was downloaded at full size.

Thumbnails are the libraries' own small previews (256 px), stored beside this page in wood_files/. Current-look thumbnails are 256 px copies of the game's colour picture.
