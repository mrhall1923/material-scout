How this was checked. Prospects came from each library's full catalogue (Poly Haven: all textures; ambientCG: every material; both fetched 16 Oct), chosen by eye from the thumbnails; the 10–11 Oct picks were kept where nothing better turned up. The licence line was read from each prospect's own page on 2026-10-16: every Poly Haven page shows “CC0 License” (and declares “CC0 1.0 Universal – public domain dedication, no attribution required”); every ambientCG page says “All assets are released under the Creative Commons CC0 license…”. ambientCG's technique and dimensions are copied from the page's Technique and Dimensions fields (the site spells procedural “Prodedural”). Poly Haven's real size is its dimensions field, with the page's “… wide” line beside it; its textures are scanned from real surfaces. Download sizes are the libraries' own file listings (Poly Haven's file API, ambientCG's download list); nothing was downloaded but the 256 px previews.

Thumbnails are ambientCG's own small previews (256 px) and, for Poly Haven, our 256 px copy of the texture's CC0 colour map (Poly Haven's previews are not CC0), stored beside this page in metal_files/. Current-look thumbnails are 256 px copies of the game's colour picture. The 3D viewer lives in viewer/ and reads copies of the game's map files (same bytes, checksums shown).
