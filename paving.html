    How this was checked. Candidates came from each library's full catalogue (Poly Haven: all textures; ambientCG: every material), matched by eye against the thumbnails. The licence line was read from each prospect's own page: every Poly Haven page shows “CC0 License” beside the author credit (and declares “CC0 1.0 Universal – public domain dedication, no attribution required”); every ambientCG page says “All assets are released under the Creative Commons CC0 license…”. ambientCG's technique is copied from the page's Technique field (the site spells procedural “Prodedural”). Poly Haven pages have no technique field; its textures are scanned from real surfaces, and where credits name a photographer that is shown.

    Thumbnails are ambientCG's own small previews (256 px) and, for Poly Haven, our 256 px copy of the texture's CC0 colour map (Poly Haven's previews are not CC0), stored beside this page in paving_files/. Current-look thumbnails are 256 px copies of the game's colour picture. The 3D viewer lives in viewer/ and reads copies of the game's map files (same bytes, checksums shown).
