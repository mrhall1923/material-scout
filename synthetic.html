How this was checked. Both catalogues were refreshed on 2026-10-16 (Poly Haven: all textures; ambientCG: every material) and the prospects chosen by eye from their thumbnails. Each prospect's own page was fetched on 2026-10-16 and its licence line read there: every Poly Haven page shows “CC0 License” beside the author credit and declares “CC0 1.0 Universal – public domain dedication, no attribution required”; every ambientCG page says “All assets are released under the Creative Commons CC0 license…”. A page without that line would have stopped the build. ambientCG's technique is copied from the page's Technique field (the site spells procedural “Prodedural”); Poly Haven's textures are scans of real surfaces, and where the credits name a photographer that is shown.

Real size: Poly Haven's own dimensions (its page's “… wide” shown beside them); ambientCG's dimension fields, which most sets leave empty. 2K size: what tools/surface_photos.py would fetch on a pull, read from each library's file list without downloading: Poly Haven's 2K PNG maps (Diffuse, nor_gl, Rough, AO, arm, where present), ambientCG's 2K-PNG zip; the JPG equivalent is shown for scale.

Thumbnails are the libraries' own small previews (256 px), stored beside this page in synthetic_files/. Current-look thumbnails are 256 px copies of the game's colour picture. The 3D viewer lives in viewer/ and reads copies of the game's map files (same bytes, checksums shown).
