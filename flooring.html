Flooring and tile scout — CC0 prospects

Every entry of Flooring and tile in the proposed surface list of 16 Oct (docs/plans/SURFACE_LIST-proposed-2026-10-16.md, d770ab5c) that the material grid uses (HIT) or proposes (GAP), less the entries already approved or under review, with up to three CC0 prospects from Poly Haven and ambientCG. Real photos are preferred; a generated (computer-made) set is listed only when neither library has a photo of that look, and it is marked. Rows with nothing real say so, with the likely route. Built 2026-10-16 by scout flooring (the 16 Oct row set). Read-only: nothing in the game was changed and nothing was downloaded at full size.

Sizes: Real size is the width one copy of the picture covers in the real world, as the library gives it. 2K size is the download at 2K: what the project's pull (tools/surface_photos.py) would fetch — ambientCG's 2K-PNG zip, Poly Haven's 2K PNG maps — and the smaller JPG figure beside it.

3D: an ambientCG prospect's picture opens ambientCG's own 3D preview; a Poly Haven prospect's page has its 3D preview. Our offline viewer of the game's own maps is not on this public copy.
